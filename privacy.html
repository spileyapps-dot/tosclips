Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What the app accesses

GTA VI Clipper uses YouTube API Services. With your permission it uses the scope "youtube.upload" to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to your own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete any other data in your Google or YouTube account.

What is stored

The access token Google issues is stored only on the computer where the app runs. The app has no server and does not store any of your data elsewhere.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or send your data to anyone. Videos are only sent to YouTube when you upload them.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on your computer also removes access.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
